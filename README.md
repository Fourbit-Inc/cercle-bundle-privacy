# Cercle Bundle Privacy Policy

Fourbit Inc. provides Cercle Bundle for Shopify merchants. This policy covers the information Cercle Bundle uses to apply bundle discounts and operate its settings in Shopify Admin.

## Information used by the app

When a merchant installs or uses Cercle Bundle, Shopify supplies the shop identifier, granted permissions, and authorization credentials needed to manage bundle discounts. Shopify can also supply staff account details, such as a name, email address, and user identifier, with an online session. The app stores session information to authenticate requests and keep access scoped to the authorized shop.

The app keeps a shop-level setup record and stores discount settings on the app-managed discount in Shopify. During checkout, the Shopify Function checks the relevant cart lines and bundle details within Shopify. The app server does not store buyer carts, orders, or payment details.

## Deletion and privacy requests

The app uses compliance webhooks from Shopify to handle data requests. On a Shopify shop deletion request, Cercle Bundle deletes the app session and setup records for that shop from its database. If you contact Fourbit Inc., we use the information you share to respond to your request.

Contact <support@bestselection.pk> for a privacy question or to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held by Cercle Bundle.
